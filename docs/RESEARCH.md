# Alternatives Research — Verdict: Continue TrendMill (July 2026)

Deep-research run: 5 search angles, 22 sources fetched, 64 claims extracted,
25 adversarially verified (3 independent votes each) → 16 confirmed, 1 refuted,
8 unverified (verification hit a rate limit; treat as plausible-but-unconfirmed).

## Ranked options for this profile (solo, ~$10/mo infra, LLM-heavy, ~5 min/day)

### 1. AI digital templates on Gumroad/Etsy (TrendMill) — ✅ CONTINUE
- Etsy **officially permits** AI-generated items based on the seller's own
  prompts, with a **mandatory AI disclosure** in the listing description
  ([Etsy policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity/), confirmed 3-0).
- No capital at risk; costs match the ~$10/mo budget; fully automatable with a
  human approval gate — the only option compatible with all constraints.
- Reality check (single-source, unverified — the related "$72/mo median
  creator" claim was *refuted* 0-2): ~44% of Gumroad products may earn $0 and
  median lifetime revenue per product is low; revenue is heavily concentrated
  ([InsightRaider](https://insightraider.com/en/state-of-gumroad-2026)).
- Winner patterns worth adopting: niche specificity, pricing **above $20**,
  4.4+ ratings, recurring revenue, **email-first distribution**.
- Core insight across all angles: *AI commoditized building; distribution is
  the binding constraint.* The machine must grow a distribution lever
  (Gumroad followers/email, Etsy search) — not just list products.

### 2. Polymarket trading bot — ❌ REJECT (trading), 🟡 tools niche as product category
- 84.1% of 2.5M wallets are net losers; only 2% ever made >$1,000 total;
  0.033% made >$100k ([on-chain study](https://sergeenkov.com/polymarket-profitability/), 3-0).
- Only 0.015% sustain income-level profits 4 months running; of ~6,600
  wallets averaging >$5k/mo, only 172 stayed active >1 year (3-0).
- Top 1% of winners capture 76.5% of all profits; winners are liquidity-providing
  market-makers — a naive signal bot is structurally the losing side
  ([SSRN 588M-trade study](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6443103), 3-0).
- Capital at risk contradicts the near-zero-budget constraint.
- Picks-and-shovels (selling tools/trackers TO traders) is saturated
  (~27 AI agents, 25+ bots, 35+ analytics tools catalogued) but structurally
  sane — viable as a **TrendMill product niche** (trader spreadsheets/trackers),
  not a separate machine.

### 3. Programmatic SEO / affiliate — ❌ REJECT as standalone
- Controlled experiment: 3 AI affiliate sites ranked and got ~200 in-market
  clicks/mo each, then **went to zero** in Google's first spam update
  ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-spam-update-ai-affiliate-sites-seo-experiment-470168), confirmed).
- Verdict from the experiment: affiliate works as a monetization layer on an
  existing audience, not as a growth engine; single-channel dependence on
  Google organic is existential platform risk (3-0).

### 4. Faceless YouTube automation — ❌ REJECT as primary
- July 15, 2025: YPP "repetitious content" renamed **"inauthentic content"**,
  targeting mass-produced content — AI use per se stays monetizable
  ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-clarifies-monetization-update-inauthentic-repeated-content/752892/), 3-0).
- The exact zero-touch pipeline (LLM script → AI voice → stock footage →
  auto-upload) is specifically what the policy targets; enforcement is
  channel-level. Early 2026: 16 major channels (~4.7B views, ~$10M/yr)
  removed from YPP (journal claims, unverified).
- Practitioner case (12 channels/2 years): first 6 never reached monetization;
  3 monetized channels all lost monetization within 4 days via one strike on a
  shared AdSense ID; ~$500/mo editor costs at peak (unverified, single source).
- Incompatible with 5 min/day + $10/mo; catastrophic correlated platform risk.

### 5. Micro-SaaS — 🟡 LATER, not now
- Building is cheap (a complete AI micro-SaaS shipped in 9 days for ~$200),
  but median first-launch revenue is $0 without distribution (Show HN: 1 point,
  0 signups) — same lesson: distribution, not product, is the constraint.

## Adjustments adopted / proposed for TrendMill
1. **Etsy expansion (M5+)**: allowed, but every listing must disclose AI use.
2. **Pricing test**: spec says $9–19; evidence favors testing >$20 — proposed
   as an owner decision (config change), not applied silently.
3. **Distribution lever**: enable Gumroad email/followers; consider a free
   lead-magnet template to build a list (future milestone).
4. **Niche idea**: prediction-market trader tools (P&L trackers, bankroll
   calculators) as a TrendMill product category — picks-and-shovels demand
   with zero trading risk.
5. **Expectation setting**: median product ≈ $0–low; the machine's edge is
   volume at near-zero marginal cost + the weekly learning loop killing losers.
